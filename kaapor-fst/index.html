Ka'apor fst demo 🌳

Interactive morphological analyser for Ka'apor (Urubu-Ka'apor), a Tupi-Guarani language of Brasil. Implements the Mirror Principle and DM Impoverishment as a finite-state transducer evaluated against a Universal Dependencies treebank.
